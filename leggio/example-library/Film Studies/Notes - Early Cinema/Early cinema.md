# Early cinema. Notes

## The first audiences

Fairgrounds, music halls and travelling showmen: cinema began as one attraction among many. A program mixed short films with songs, magic acts and lectures.

## Key points

- 1895: the first paid public screenings.
- Films last one or two minutes: a train arriving, workers leaving a factory, a short gag.
- A live lecturer or a pianist often stands next to the screen.
- By the 1910s, longer films and dedicated cinemas change who goes and why.

## Words to know

- **Actuality:** a short film of a real scene, without a story.
- **Nickelodeon:** a small cinema with a five cent ticket.
- **Intertitle:** a card with text between two shots.

**In one sentence:** before it told stories, cinema was a show about the wonder of moving images.
